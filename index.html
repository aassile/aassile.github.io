Sonar Mine Detection — Mines vs. Rocks Classification

Python scikit-learn Extra Trees Plotly pytest

End-to-end binary classification pipeline on the UCI Sonar dataset, distinguishing underwater mines from rocks using 60 acoustic frequency-band energy readings. Compared four models — Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting, and a neural net — with the tuned Extra Trees winning at 92.9% test accuracy and 0.982 ROC–AUC. Includes permutation-importance interpretability, K-Means clustering, and synthetic stress-testing. Packaged into importable src/ modules with a reproducible notebook, a pytest suite, and CI.
